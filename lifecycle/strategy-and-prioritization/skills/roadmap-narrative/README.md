# Roadmap Narrative

## The problem

A prioritized backlog and a roadmap are not the same artifact. Handing stakeholders a ranked list or a Gantt chart with dates answers "what and when" but not "why this order" - and that gap is usually where roadmap credibility breaks down, especially when priorities shift later and no one remembers the original logic.

## The approach

This skill takes an already-prioritized backlog and surfaces the connective narrative: why each item's timing makes sense, what it unlocks for later items, and what's deliberately excluded. It refuses to invent a strategic theme the PM hasn't validated, and it keeps committed items visually distinct from directional ones so a "Later" phase doesn't get read as a promise.

## Where it's used

Turning a `rice-scoring-copilot` output (or any pre-ranked backlog) into something presentable to executives, a board, or a specific customer - reusable across audiences by adjusting the detail level.

## Try it

Bring a prioritized list and answer a couple of questions about audience and strategic theme; it will draft the narrative and flag anything it can't justify from what you've told it.
