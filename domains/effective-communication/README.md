# Effective Communication as a PM

**Course window:** 06 Jun - 12 Jun

Covers: why communication is core to PM work, principles of effective communication (audience, objective, content, channel, cadence), layers of PM communication, writing clear PRDs, stakeholder communication in practice (immediate, upward, outer-circle), key product communication docs, and storytelling/case practice.

## Contents

| Item | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [`prd-copilot`](prd-copilot) | Skill | Works section-by-section with a PM to produce a complete, consistent PRD - the flagship example of "Writing Clear PRDs" from this module |
| [`acceptance-criteria-auditor`](acceptance-criteria-auditor) | Skill | Audits pasted-in acceptance criteria for vague or untestable language and rewrites them into strict Given/When/Then form |
