# 03 · Problem Discovery, Market and User Research

**Course window:** 16 May - 22 May

Covers: what problem discovery is and why it matters, key components of market research, validating ideas with market data, TAM-SAM-SOM and Porter's Five Forces, evaluating competitive landscapes, what user research is and why it's needed, user research approaches, customer segments and personas, and turning research into a strategy pitch.
